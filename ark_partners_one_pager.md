## Go-To-Market: Pricing & Positioning

### The Core Insight

Most businesses know they need to be using AI. They're just not sure how, where to start, or who to trust. That's exactly where we come in. We've done this across dozens of businesses and we know what works.

The majority of prospects are **problem-aware but not solution-aware** — they know AI matters, they feel behind, but they don't know what to buy or who to call. Our base offering is designed to meet them exactly there: low barrier, immediate value, and a natural on-ramp into bigger engagements.

### Base Offering: AI Foundation — $1,500/mo

This is our rinse-and-repeat foundation for every client because the ROI is immediate and obvious:

- **ARK Chat in their Slack** — their own AI employee they talk to like a team member. Voice notes, text, whatever. She handles drafts, research, scheduling, analysis, and the hundred small things eating their hours.
- **Their Company Brain (MCP Server)** — we build a centralized knowledge server loaded with their SOPs, brand docs, contracts, data, and business context. Every time they open AI, it already knows who they are and what they're working on. No re-explaining. No switching between accounts.
- **Data Source Connections** — we plug into their existing stack (Google, Slack, CRM, analytics, whatever they're running) so ARK can actually see their business.
- **Audit + Recommendations** — once we're connected, we run a full analysis and surface exactly where AI can move the needle — quick wins and longer-term plays and display in an analysis dashboard for them. 
- **Dedicated Slack channel** with our team for ongoing support, optimization, and training.

**First month trial for base offering: $1,000** ($500 off) — we get them fully set up, connected, and running. They see what's possible before they even hit month two.

### Expansion: Custom Projects — $2,000–5,000+/mo

Some clients show up already problem-aware and know they want to jump into a bigger package — outbound campaigns, custom dashboards, workflow automation, a full CRM. Great. We meet them where they are and scope accordingly.

But most people don't start there. They start with the foundation, see what's possible, and then the scope grows naturally. No pressure, no big upfront commitment. We deliver, they see the value, and we expand from there, but we get them in at a base level as a paying client that we can upsell from there.
